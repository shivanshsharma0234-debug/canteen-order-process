
# Business Requirements Document

## 1. Business Objective
Create a clearer and more efficient canteen ordering journey by reducing unnecessary manual coordination and improving order-status visibility.

## 2. Problem Statement
The existing journey can involve repeated communication, manual queue handling and limited visibility of order progress, particularly during busy periods.

## 3. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Need |
|---|---|
| Students / Customers | Fast ordering and clear status |
| Canteen Staff | Accurate, manageable order queue |
| Canteen Management | Better process visibility |
| Business Analyst | Clear requirements and measurable improvements |

## 4. In Scope
- Menu browsing
- Item selection
- Cart
- Order confirmation
- Order status
- Preparation workflow
- Pickup notification
- Process reporting

## 5. Out of Scope
- Supplier management
- Inventory procurement
- Payroll
- Vendor accounting

## 6. Business Requirements

**BR-01** — Customers need a structured way to select menu items.

**BR-02** — Customers need to review their order before submission.

**BR-03** — Staff need a clear view of incoming orders.

**BR-04** — Customers need order confirmation and status visibility.

**BR-05** — The process should reduce unnecessary manual handoffs.

**BR-06** — Management should be able to review process performance.

## 7. Functional Requirements

**FR-01** Select item and quantity.  
**FR-02** Add/remove items from cart.  
**FR-03** Display order total.  
**FR-04** Submit order.  
**FR-05** Generate order reference.  
**FR-06** Staff can view order queue.  
**FR-07** Staff can update status.  
**FR-08** Customer can view current status.  
**FR-09** Customer receives ready-for-pickup indication.

## 8. Success Criteria

- Fewer unnecessary ordering steps
- Clearer order status
- Less repeated communication
- Better visibility for staff
- Better customer experience
